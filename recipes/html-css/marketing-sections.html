Questions before you start

No. They get an email with the invoice and can pay by card or bank transfer without signing up.

Can I export my invoices if I leave?

Yes. Export everything as CSV or PDF at any time, including after you cancel.

What happens when the trial ends?

Nothing is charged until you choose a plan. Your invoices stay readable, and reminders pause until you do.
