Polish, by dimension

  • Hierarchy and focal point3 / 4 (was 2)

    Tinted Outstanding card and New invoice button give a clear focal point and the first-run hero leads well, but the page title (about 30px) and KPI values (about 28px) sit at nearly the same size, and the 6-month total in the chart header competes with the KPI row. dashboard-index-1440-light.png, dashboard-index-state-new-1440-light.png

  • Typography3 / 4 (was 2)

    One well-chosen grotesk with a clear scale, tabular right-aligned amounts and tracked uppercase table headers. Title and KPI sizes are too close, and small grey secondary lines (invoice IDs, captions) are faint. dashboard-index-1440-light.png, dashboard-index-375-light.png

  • Spacing and layout3 / 4 (was 1)

    Spacing scale and shared edges are consistent, and the nav wraps rather than clipping at 375. In the new-account state at 1440, the half-width checklist card leaves a large empty right column, so the layout looks unbalanced. dashboard-index-state-new-1440-light.png, dashboard-index-375-light.png

  • Colour4 / 4 (was 2)

    Restrained teal brand on tinted neutrals, used sparingly: the primary button, the active nav item and the focal card. Red and green carry meaning only (overdue, trend). Dark theme is designed, not inverted: the chart is re-tinted and the partial-month bar inverts sensibly. dashboard-index-1440-light.png, dashboard-index-1440-dark.png

  • Surfaces, depth and shape3 / 4 (was 2)

    Quiet, consistent layering with one radius, hairline borders, inner tinted tiles and no shadows, and it reads in both themes. No overlay was captured, so popover and dialog layers cannot be judged. dashboard-index-state-new-1440-dark.png, dashboard-index-1440-dark.png

  • Component craft3 / 4 (was 2)

    Loading, first-run and error states are all designed, and table, badges and mobile list are well built. But the dark-mode skeletons are almost invisible against the card, the KPI skeleton cards are shorter than the loaded cards (layout shift), and no overlay or hover/focus state was captured. dashboard-index-state-loading-1440-dark.png, dashboard-index-state-loading-1440-light.png, dashboard-index-state-error-1440-light.png

  • Imagery, icons and assets3 / 4 (was 2)

    A single consistent line-icon set, sized to text (status badges, plus, chevrons, theme toggle), and a simple logo mark. No product imagery, and the favicon and social image were not visible in the captures. dashboard-index-1440-light.png, dashboard-index-state-new-1440-light.png

  • Motion and feedbackn/a

    Still screenshots only; the page could not be operated, so motion and feedback were not observed.

  • Copy and content3 / 4 (was 2)

    Specific, calm voice ('Revenue didn't load. The connection dropped... Your data is safe.', checklist benefits). However, the error state still says '12 invoices · newest first' under a section whose invoices failed to load. dashboard-index-state-error-1440-light.png, dashboard-index-state-new-1440-light.png

  • Consistency3 / 4 (was 2)

    Same header, cards, buttons and voice across all states and both widths. Small drift: the same action is labelled 'New invoice' on the dashboard and 'Create invoice' on first run, and that state drops the header button. dashboard-index-1440-light.png, dashboard-index-state-new-1440-light.png

The returning home leads with outstanding and overdue amounts plus direct actions (View open invoices, Send reminders). dashboard-index-state-new-1440-light.png, dashboard-index-state-new-375-light.png, dashboard-index-1440-light.png
